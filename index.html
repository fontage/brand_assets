Logo Usage Guidelines

Official Brand Asset Usage & Protection Policy

Thank you for your interest in our brand. Our logo represents our company’s identity, reputation, and values. To maintain brand consistency and protect our visual identity, all individuals, organizations, partners, developers, and third parties must follow the guidelines outlined in this document.

By downloading, displaying, or using our logo, you agree to comply with the following guidelines.

1. Ownership and Intellectual Property

  • Our company logo, including its symbols, graphics, typography, and associated visual elements, is the intellectual property of our company, subject to applicable intellectual property laws.
  • All rights, ownership, and interests in the logo remain with the company unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Providing publicly accessible logo files does not transfer ownership or grant unrestricted usage rights.
  • No individual or organization may claim ownership, authorship, or exclusive rights over our logo.

2. Authorized Logo Usage

Our logo may only be used for purposes explicitly authorized by our company.

Permitted usage may include:

  • Displaying our logo when referencing our company in legitimate informational or editorial content, subject to applicable permissions and these guidelines.
  • Using the logo in approved partnership, collaboration, or sponsorship materials.
  • Displaying the logo on platforms or materials where our company has explicitly granted permission.
  • Using official logo assets for approved promotional or marketing activities.

Important: Public availability of our logo does not automatically grant permission to use it. Commercial, promotional, partnership, or other non-editorial usage requires prior written authorization.

3. Logo Modification Restrictions

Modifying, altering, or manipulating our official logo is strictly prohibited.

The following modifications are not permitted:

  • No Color Changes: Do not change, replace, adjust, or customize the original logo colors.
  • No Distortion: Do not stretch, squeeze, rotate, skew, or otherwise distort the logo’s original proportions.
  • No Resizing Distortion: Always maintain the original aspect ratio when resizing the logo.
  • No Graphic Modifications: Do not add, remove, replace, or modify any graphical elements.
  • No Typography Changes: Do not alter the logo’s original typography, font, lettering, or spacing.
  • No Effects: Do not apply unauthorized shadows, gradients, outlines, filters, textures, or visual effects.
  • No Cropping: Do not crop, cut off, or hide any part of the logo.
  • No Rebranding: Do not incorporate our logo into another organization’s identity or create modified versions that suggest a different brand.

Any form of unauthorized modification, misuse, or intentional vandalism of our logo is prohibited.

4. Use of Official Logo Files

To maintain consistency and prevent incorrect representations:

  • Always obtain logo files from our official website or authorized distribution channels.
  • Use the original, unmodified logo files.
  • Do not recreate the logo manually.
  • Do not download logo files from unofficial sources when official assets are available.
  • Do not convert the logo into formats that compromise its quality or visual appearance.
  • Do not use low-resolution or pixelated versions when official high-quality assets are available.

If you require a different logo format, color variation, or background-compatible version, contact our team for assistance.

5. Prohibited Usage

The following activities are strictly prohibited without explicit written authorization:

  1. Using our logo for commercial activities, products, or services in a way that implies endorsement or affiliation.
  2. Using our logo to promote illegal, misleading, fraudulent, harmful, or deceptive activities.
  3. Using our logo in offensive, defamatory, inappropriate, or misleading content.
  4. Creating fake, modified, or unofficial versions of our logo.
  5. Using our logo to impersonate our company or misrepresent an official relationship.
  6. Registering our logo or a confusingly similar version as a trademark, business identity, domain name, or social media identity.
  7. Selling, redistributing, sublicensing, or claiming ownership of our official logo assets.
  8. Removing copyright notices, ownership information, or other identifying information associated with our brand assets.

6. Brand Representation and Misuse

Our logo must never be displayed in a manner that could damage our company’s reputation, misrepresent our services, or create confusion about our identity.

You must not:

  • Suggest that your organization is officially affiliated with our company without authorization.
  • Suggest that our company endorses a product, service, individual, or organization without written approval.
  • Use our logo in a way that creates false partnerships, sponsorships, or business relationships.
  • Associate our brand with misleading statements or unauthorized advertisements.

7. Permission and Authorization

If you wish to use our logo for purposes beyond permitted informational or editorial references, you must obtain prior written permission from our company.

Your request should include:

  • Your name or organization.
  • The intended purpose of logo usage.
  • Where and how the logo will be displayed.
  • The expected duration of usage.
  • Any relevant promotional, commercial, or partnership details.

Permission must be obtained through our official communication channels.

Approval for one specific usage does not automatically authorize other forms of usage.

8. Reporting Unauthorized Usage

We take unauthorized logo usage, brand impersonation, and intellectual property violations seriously.

If you discover:

  • Unauthorized use of our logo.
  • Modified or distorted versions of our logo.
  • Fake accounts or websites impersonating our company.
  • Misleading promotional materials using our brand identity.
  • Any form of logo vandalism or misuse.

Please report the incident through our official contact channels and include relevant screenshots, URLs, or supporting information.

We reserve the right to investigate reported incidents and take appropriate action.

9. Enforcement and Legal Action

Any unauthorized use, modification, distribution, or misuse of our logo may result in appropriate action.

Where applicable and subject to relevant laws, our company reserves the right to:

  • Request immediate removal of unauthorized logo usage.
  • Request correction of modified or misleading brand representations.
  • Revoke previously granted usage permissions.
  • Submit intellectual property complaints to relevant platforms.
  • Pursue appropriate legal remedies against infringement or unauthorized commercial exploitation.

Nothing in this document limits any rights or remedies available to our company under applicable intellectual property laws.

10. Updates to These Guidelines

We reserve the right to update, modify, or revise these logo usage guidelines at any time.

Users and organizations are responsible for reviewing the latest version before using our brand assets.

Continued use of our logo following an update constitutes acceptance of the revised guidelines, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

For logo usage permissions, partnership inquiries, brand-related questions, or reports of unauthorized usage, please contact us through our official communication channels.
